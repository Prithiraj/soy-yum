About this website preview

This is an independent design preview for Soy Yum’s Hindustan Park restaurant. It is not represented as an owner-approved official website. The calling and directions links connect to publicly listed business details; this site does not take bookings, payments or orders.

Photography is preview-only, not cleared for commercial launch. These are actual photographs published in Soy Yum’s restaurant listing, not generated food or stock interiors. A listing or a brand watermark does not establish ownership or grant permission to reuse an image. All photographs and the logo require appropriate rightsholder approval or replacement before an official commercial launch.

Photographs are linked to their source below. Brand marks within food photographs have not been removed. Food photography is illustrative and is not a promise that a specific item is currently available.

Privacy & external services

This site does not operate an analytics tracker, use marketing cookies, or collect reservation details. Images are served from the site’s deployment. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; your browser sends the normal connection information needed for that request. GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes requests under its own policies.

Calling links open your device’s phone application. Maps, social profiles and restaurant-listing links open external services with their own policies. The “Copy address” control writes only the public restaurant address to your clipboard after you activate it. No clipboard contents are read.

Local interface events are available for a future consent-reviewed analytics integration, but are not transmitted anywhere by this implementation.
